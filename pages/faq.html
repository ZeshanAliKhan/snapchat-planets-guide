Snapchat Planets FAQ

Is Pluto part of Snapchat planets?

No. Snapchat’s official Friend Solar System order ends at Neptune.

What does Mercury mean?

It represents the closest rank in the Best Friends lineup tied to the Solar System feature.

Why can’t I see the Solar System?

For first-time Snapchat+ subscribers, the feature can be off by default and may need to be enabled.

Does a planet prove how close the friendship is in real life?

No. It reflects interaction frequency inside Snapchat, not the full reality of the relationship.
