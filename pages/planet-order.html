The official Snapchat planets order

This is the part that gets repeated incorrectly all over the internet. Snapchat’s own support information describes an eight-planet order, not a nine-planet nostalgia chart.

  1. Mercury - closest rank
  2. Venus
  3. Earth
  4. Mars
  5. Jupiter
  6. Saturn
  7. Uranus
  8. Neptune - eighth spot
Important correction:

Pluto is not part of Snapchat’s official Friend Solar System list. If you see Pluto in a ranking graphic, it is almost certainly fan lore or recycled misinformation.

The feature is tied to Best Friends in Snapchat+. It maps your relative interaction ranking inside the app, with each planet standing in for a position in that top-eight group.
